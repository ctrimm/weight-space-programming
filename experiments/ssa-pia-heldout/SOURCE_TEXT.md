## PIA definition (excerpt)

> The "primary insurance amount" (PIA) is the benefit (before
> rounding down to next lower whole dollar) a person would receive
> if he/she elects to begin receiving retirement benefits at his/her
> normal retirement age. At this age, the benefit is neither reduced
> for early retirement nor increased for delayed retirement.

## PIA formula bend points (excerpt)

> The PIA is the sum of three separate percentages of portions of
> average indexed monthly earnings. The portions depend on the year
> in which a worker attains age 62, becomes disabled before age 62,
> or dies before attaining age 62.
>
> For 2027 these portions are the first $1,340, the amount between
> $1,340 and $8,077, and the amount over $8,077. These dollar amounts
> are the "bend points" of the 2027 PIA formula.

## PIA formula (excerpt)

> For an individual who first becomes eligible for old-age insurance
> benefits or disability insurance benefits in 2027, or who dies in
> 2027 before becoming eligible for benefits, his/her PIA will be
> the sum of: (a) 90 percent of the first $1,340 of his/her average
> indexed monthly earnings, plus (b) 32 percent of his/her average
> indexed monthly earnings over $1,340 and through $8,077, plus
> (c) 15 percent of his/her average indexed monthly earnings over
> $8,077. We round this amount to the next lower multiple of $.10
> if it is not already a multiple of $.10.
